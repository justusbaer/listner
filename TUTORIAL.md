# Listner Tutorial & Guide

Welcome to **Listner**! This application is designed to securely capture your thoughts using voice-to-text, organize them automatically using AI, and turn them into structured projects.

This document covers the key user journeys, explains how to use the app, and answers frequently asked questions.

---

## 🚀 Key User Journeys

### 1. Capturing a Thought
The core feature of Listner is quickly capturing your thoughts as they happen.

1. Open the app and ensure you are on the **Capture** tab.
2. Tap the large **"Tap to Record"** button.
3. Speak your thought, idea, or task clearly into your microphone.
4. Tap **"Tap to Stop"** when you are finished.
5. The AI will process your audio, transcribe it, assign it a category (e.g., Idea, Work, Personal), and save it along with your current location. You'll see a brief conversational confirmation message on the screen!

### 2. Reviewing Recent Captures (Inbox)
Notes that haven't been assigned to a project yet live in your inbox.

1. On the **Capture** tab, tap **"View Recent Captures"**.
2. Here, you will see a list of all your unassigned notes, including their category, date, and location tag.
3. You can review your notes or delete any mistakes by tapping **"Delete"**.

### 3. Automated Project Creation (The Orchestrator)
Listner has an intelligent "Orchestrator" that works in the background to help you make sense of your notes.

1. When you capture a new note, the Orchestrator waits for about 15 seconds to see if you have more related thoughts.
2. If it detects a pattern or enough content in your unassigned notes, it will automatically propose a new project. 
3. You will see a status indicator in the **Projects** tab (e.g., "AI is thinking..." or "New project proposed!").

### 4. Manually Starting a Project
You don't have to wait for the Orchestrator; you can group notes into a project yourself.

1. On the **Capture** tab, tap **"View Recent Captures"**.
2. Tap on multiple notes to select them (they will be highlighted).
3. Once you've selected the relevant notes, tap **"Start New Project"** at the bottom of the screen.
4. The AI will process these notes and generate a structured project draft.

### 5. Reviewing and Refining Projects
Projects give structure to your raw thoughts (e.g., forming them into a Diary, a Brainstorm, or Chapters).

1. Navigate to the **Projects** tab.
2. If the Orchestrator proposed a project, it will be highlighted in yellow. Tap it, then choose to **Accept Project** or **Discard Proposal**.
3. Inside a project, you can view the Summary, Next Steps, and structured content.
4. **Discuss with Expert:** At the bottom of the project page, you can chat (via text or voice) with an AI Project Expert. Use this to refine the scope, ask questions, or expand on the ideas within the project!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do I need an internet connection to record notes?**
A: Yes. Listner relies on cloud-based AI to transcribe your audio, categorize it, and save it to your secure database.

**Q: Why does the app ask for my location?**
A: Listner tags your notes with your current location (e.g., "New York, US" or coordinates) to give context to where you had a specific thought. This helps you remember *where* you were when an idea struck you. 

**Q: How does the AI group my notes automatically?**
A: After you capture a note, the Orchestrator waits briefly. It looks at your recent unassigned notes and uses AI to determine if they relate to a common theme. If they do, it drafts a project proposal for you to review.

**Q: Can I add a new note to an existing project?**
A: Currently, the "Add to Existing" functionality is marked as "coming soon." Right now, you can create new projects from notes, or refine existing projects by chatting with the Project Expert.

**Q: What are Archetypes?**
A: When the AI creates a project, it assigns it an "Archetype" to format the data best. Common archetypes include **Brainstorm** (showing Pros, Cons, and a Thesis), **Diary** (a chronological list of entries), or **Chapters** (structured sections of text).

**Q: How do I talk to the Project Expert using voice?**
A: Inside a project, scroll to the bottom. Tap the **"Tap to Record Message"** button, speak your question or instruction, and tap to stop. The AI will transcribe your message, send it to the expert, and reply. If your device supports it, the AI will also speak the reply out loud!
